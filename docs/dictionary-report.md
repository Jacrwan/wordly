## What the numbers mean for the app

- **Korean meanings.** Common words are well covered: 93% of the top 1,000 words have Korean, directly or borrowed from their base word. Coverage falls to 62% across the top 30,000. Words without Korean trigger the Gemini gap-fill in step 3 on first lookup, and the result is cached.
- **Example sentences with Korean.** This is the big gap, as expected: only 11,480 English–Korean pairs exist on Tatoeba. 81% of the top 1,000 words have 2 or more pairs, but only 13% of the top 30,000 do. Gemini will fill examples for most words outside the top few thousand.
- **Pronunciation audio.** 97% of the top 3,000 words have a native recording; the rest fall back to Web Speech.
- **Phrases.** 7,368 multi-word entries ("give up", "by the way") were added because they have Korean translations. A phrase's rank comes from its rarest word, so "by the way" is Beginner and "take into account" is Intermediate.

## Known data quirks

- Some Wiktionary translations sit on the wrong sense (e.g. "house" → 의원 on the "place of business" sense). The 쉬운 설명 note and AI fill help offset this.
- A few English loanwords from Korean show up in the Korean index (월세 → "wolse") when no better English entry has that translation.
